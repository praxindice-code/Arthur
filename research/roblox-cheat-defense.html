Explore Roblox cheat defense over time

A sourced timeline of Roblox’s anti-cheat response, paired with a hands-on model of server-side gameplay signals.

What this tool actually does

1 · Read the record
Browse dated Roblox announcements below. These are sourced facts, not model inputs.

2 · Play a scenario
Step through invented server-side gameplay events. No player or Roblox account is connected.

3 · Watch the estimate
Zig checks the event rows, then Rust updates a hand-configured sequential model. Its percentage is not calibrated for real players.

4 · Change the alert rule
The slider and consecutive-event setting change when an alert appears. Bayesian tuning is optional future work, not part of this run.

This is a research demo, not a live anti-cheat, breach detector, or evidence for banning someone.
